# Design

Mode: Converge
Kind: application

The PM and CRM app: projects, tasks, the Inbox (email and meetings), leads, contacts, team, the client portal. Dark first, with a light theme a person can switch to. Drafted from the code on 2026-11-17.

## Rules (binding)

Decided by the Owner on 2026-11-26. Every screen, and every person or AI that builds one, follows these. **If a screen needs something these rules do not cover, stop and ask; never invent a new pattern, size, color or component.** `npm run check:design` fails on the banned markup below, and review checks the rest. The live pattern page (`/design`, every pattern with the real components, light and dark) is the picture of these rules.

**The look.** The reference screens are the Inbox thread, a project's Tasks board, the project Overview, the Initiatives tab, the side panel (editing a project) and Settings > Templates. Liquid glass on near-black, calm cards with hairline rows, small sentence-case pills, one teal primary action per area. Match them; anything that looks unlike them is wrong even if it follows a rule.

1. **Where a record opens.** View or edit any record (task, project, initiative, template, schedule, contact, lead, member, group, API key, invoice, retainer, run, activity item) in the side panel, `TaskPanelShell` with `PanelHeader`. A centered `Modal` only for a short decision, a confirm, or a short create that ends in a secret or a list row (a new API key, a webhook endpoint). A full page only for a guided setup that creates something with its own page (an agent, a project) and for a record's own page reached by link. Never a panel inside a panel; a picker inside a panel is a `Popover` that stays inside the panel's width.
2. **Headers.** Page title: `Header`, always, naming the page (a Settings page shows its own name, never "Settings"). Card header inside the app: the calm recipe (`CardHeader`: 16px muted icon, `text-sm` semibold title, one action on the right). On Settings pages only: `SettingsSectionHeader`. Panels: `PanelHeader` (title and close; no uppercase eyebrow). Headings use the type scale only (`text-[11px]`, `text-xs`, `text-sm`, `text-base`, `text-lg`, page title); never `text-[15px]` or another one-off size. No uppercase eyebrow labels anywhere; uppercase is only for `GroupLabel` list group labels.
3. **Tabs and filters.** Underline `Tabs` for a page's sections. `SubNav` (segmented) for two to four views or states. One `FilterButton` that opens a panel for anything more, with active filters counted on the button. Never a row of `Select`s above a list, never pill chips as filters, never two selector styles stacked.
4. **Lists.** `DataTable` for records with columns (people, keys, runs, contacts, invoices); it becomes a card per row on phones. A row list in a card (`divide-y` rows: icon or avatar, title, meta line, actions right, one `RowActionsMenu`) for queues and libraries. A card per item only for a few rich items that show progress (initiatives). Agents are a list, not cards. Every long list ends with `ShowMore` ("Show 24 more of 76"); never "Load more", "Show all" or page numbers.
5. **Empty states.** `EmptyState`, sized to the area it fills: a small muted icon, one line saying what goes here, one line on how it fills, and the one action if this person can take it. `variant="filtered"` says nothing matched and offers Clear filters; `variant="error"` says what failed and offers Try again. Never a blank area, a bare grey sentence, an oversized card or a narrow card floating in the page.
6. **Undo, not confirm.** Anything a person can get back acts at once and leaves one `UndoLine` where it happened ("Moved to Trash. Undo"); moving to Trash is always this. `ConfirmDialog` only for what cannot be undone: revoking a key, deleting for good, sending to people outside the app, applying an update. A confirm title is a sentence-case question ("Revoke this key?").
7. **Status labels.** `Pill` only: 11px, fully rounded, sentence case, a dot or small icon, soft fill, one of five tones (neutral, good, waiting, problem, info). Never a hand-rolled tinted span, never a taller chip, never Title Case ("In progress", not "In Progress"). Money and counts are plain text, never status-colored.
8. **Saving.** Switches save at once (with Undo where it matters). A form gets `SaveBar`: pinned to the bottom of the panel or screen, shown only once something changed, Discard then Save on the right, and the discard guard (`useDiscardGuard`) on leaving. Never a Save button floating mid-card or at the bottom left.
9. **Page width.** Full width for boards, tables and list plus detail. One reading width (`page-read`, 880px, tabs included) for feeds, forms, Settings, My Tasks and Needs you. Never a small card centered in an empty page.
10. **Permissions.** `PermissionGrid` everywhere a person picks what someone or something may do (hiring an agent, a new key, editing a key, Roles & permissions, Connect AI): a row per area, columns View, View all and Manage, a column title picks the whole column, cells this person cannot give are locked with the reason on hover, and special powers sit below as a short list with one plain sentence each. Logic in `src/lib/permission-grid.ts`; never a separate checkbox list or three-state boxes.
11. **One of each.** One `Avatar` (the same tone and initials for a person or agent everywhere), one `IconButton` (two sizes), one menu trigger (the horizontal "more" icon), one time format family (`src/lib/time-format.ts`: "5 min ago", "Yesterday", "Nov 26"), one loader per kind (`Skeleton` for content, `Loader2` inside a button only), one guided-flow family (`FlowShell`, `Stepper`, `StepFooter`, the first-run wizard included).
12. **Words.** Sentence case for every title, label and button. Placeholders without trailing dots. Busy labels without dots ("Saving"). No em dashes. Name things the way the person knows them, never by how the system is built.
13. **Light mode.** Every color comes from a token that both themes define; status ink below 300 is never used for text. A screen is not done until it reads well in light and dark, on desktop and at 390px.
14. **Cards side by side share one height.** Cards in a row stretch to the tallest; a list inside one keeps normal row height in a scroll area of its own, with any total pinned at its bottom. Rows are never spread out to fill the height.
15. **Dialogs stay short.** A title of two to four words, no subtitle unless it changes the decision, only the fields the action needs (the rest under Advanced), and one primary button that names the action, beside Cancel.
16. **Data anyone can read.** Anyone in the company, whatever their job, understands a data view on first look. A row of numbers is one `StatStrip` surface split into segments: label, value, change against the last period, and a quiet trend line; on analytics pages each segment is selectable (`onSelect`, `selected` underlined in brand) and picks what the chart below shows. Never a grid of separate boxed tiles. Each chart answers one question named in plain words above it ("Runs per day, last 30 days"); one brand color for the series in focus, muted ink for the rest; light gridlines, few axis labels, units always shown; a plain tooltip with the exact value and date. Change is green or red only when up or down has a clear good or bad meaning (spend going up is red), otherwise neutral; money and counts themselves stay plain ink. No 3D, no pie charts with more than three slices, no legend a person must decode: label lines directly. An empty or still-loading chart says what will appear there. Every number on screen can be traced to where it comes from (`data-correctness`).
17. **One rhythm, no stray rows.** Never give a link, a small button or a line of text a row of its own between sections: an action lives in the header of the card or section it acts on (`CardHeader` action slot, or the toolbar), a note lives inside its card. Filters and the date range sit in the same header as the thing they filter, not in a separate card above it. Spacing comes from one scale only: 24px between sections and cards on a page (`gap-6`), 16px inside a card (`p-4`, `lg:p-5` on wide cards), 12px between rows of controls, 8px between a label and its value. Same-level cards share one padding. A page reads as one cohesive system: the same header, tabs, filters, table, chart and icon treatment everywhere, so nothing looks like it came from a different product.
18. **Every AI setup is a guided path anyone can finish.** If the people building the system get confused, everyday users will. Setting up an agent, an assistant, a chat app or a key goes one thing per screen, says plainly when a step is optional, and ends with "It works, try it" plus the one action that proves it (for a chat bot: "Send @Bot a message in Telegram to finish", with a button that opens it). A waiting state is the next step, never a problem ("Nothing yet" is wrong). Advanced options (group mapping, reply modes, ids) stay collapsed under one "Optional" or "Advanced" line, in plain words; no ids, scopes or internal names on the main path.
19. **Use the space; never strand it.** Content is sized to what it holds: a table or grid with a few narrow columns never stretches them across a wide screen with a gulf between the label and its values (size columns to content beside the label, or give it a sensible max width, or put it where it belongs: an editor like the permissions grid opens in the side panel or a wide dialog, not across a full page). A lone card never sits in half a row with empty space beside it: it spans the row, pairs with a related card of the same height, or the page uses the reading width (Rule 9). Cohesive, never boring: one system on every page, with the signature moments (liquid glass, the brand glow on what you can act on, quiet motion) used deliberately, not stripped out.
20. **Show the real thing, not a generic icon.** Wherever a screen is about a provider or product (Telegram, Slack, Discord, Google, GitHub, Claude, ChatGPT, an email provider), show its real logo with `ProviderLogo`, never a generic chat or check icon. A state sits on the logo as a small badge in its bottom right corner: a check for connected or done, a clock for waiting, a warning for a problem. Success and empty states that confirm a connection lead with that logo and badge (for example "Telegram account linked": the Telegram logo with a green check badge), then one line of what happens next and one action.

## Tokens

All in `src/app/globals.css`, one source of truth:

- **Brand**: `--color-brand-50` to `--color-brand-700` (teal, `#5b8a8a` at 500), generated from the color an Owner picks in Settings > Workspace > Brand and injected at runtime by `ThemeProvider` (defaults in `src/lib/brand/defaults.ts`). Copper (`--color-copper`) is the one accent beside it. The brand settings hold the brand only; fonts and surfaces stay in CSS.
- **Surfaces**: `--color-surface` (page), `--color-surface-raised` (cards, modals), `--color-surface-overlay` (menus, popovers, tooltips), `--color-surface-border`. Glass: `--glass-bg`, `--glass-bg-strong`, `--glass-highlight`, `--glass-blur`.
- **Ink**: `--ink` is the RGB the white alpha utilities (`bg-white/[0.04]`, `text-zinc-*`) resolve through, so `[data-theme="light"]` remaps them without per-component work. Write `bg-white/[0.0x]` and `text-zinc-*` in components; never a hex.
- **Type**: DM Sans (`--font-sans`) for everything, DM Mono (`--font-mono`) only for codes and ids. Sizes in use: `text-[11px]` meta, `text-xs` secondary, `text-sm` body, `text-base`/`text-lg` view titles.
- **Inputs**: `--color-input-*` and `--radius-input`, `--radius-input-sm`.
- **Elevation**: `--shadow-card`, `--shadow-card-hover`, `--shadow-overlay`, `--shadow-glow` (brand-derived).
- **Status colors**: Tailwind's emerald (done, tasks), amber (waiting, reminders, needs you), red (urgent, errors), sky (replies owed), at low alpha fills (`bg-amber-500/[0.14]`) with 200 to 300 ink.

## Page anatomy

- Shell: always-dark sidebar (`src/components/layout/Sidebar.tsx`, `data-theme="dark"`), then the page body. No header bar beyond `Header.tsx`.
- A page body is a `glass-card` (rounded-xl) or a column of them. List and detail views split into a list column (320 to 380px) and a detail pane on desktop, and take turns on phones (a back button on the detail).
- A detail header: title (`h2`, semibold, tight tracking), one wrapping row of status, project chips and counts, then actions on the right in one bordered button group (`RowActionsMenu` for the rest).
- Sections inside a pane are separated by `border-white/[0.06]` hairlines, not boxes.

## Components

Shared, in `src/components/ui/`: `Button` (primary, secondary, ghost, danger; sm, md, lg), `Modal`, `ConfirmDialog` (only for what cannot be undone, Rule 6), `Tooltip`, `Popover`, `RowActionsMenu`, `Toast` (`toast()`), `Avatar`, `Badge`, `CountBadge`, `DataTable`, `StatStrip`, `Sparkline`, `SectionHeader`, `Select`, `Toggle`, `FilePreviewModal`, inputs in `ui/inputs/`.

The pieces the Rules name (each on `/design`, light and dark):

- `Pill` (`ui/Pill.tsx`, Rule 7): 11px, rounded-full, sentence case, optional `dot` or `icon`, tones `neutral`, `good`, `waiting`, `problem`, `info`. `Badge`, `StatusBadge`, `PriorityBadge`, `ProjectStatusBadge`, `TaskTypeBadge` and `TagBadge` are built on it.
- `PanelHeader` (`ui/PanelHeader.tsx`, Rules 1 and 2): title (or an editable title node) and close, with optional header actions; no eyebrow. An optional quiet `detail` line in normal case only when the panel needs context (the project a new task lands in). Every `TaskPanelShell` uses it, the task panel included.
- `Card` and `CardHeader` (`ui/Card.tsx`, Rules 2 and 17): the calm card, 16px muted icon, `text-sm` semibold title; the `action` slot holds up to two quiet actions plus the card's filters (FilterButton, a small date range Select), 8px apart, wrapping together under the title on phones. 16px inside, 20px from lg. `RailCard` is a thin wrapper.
- `EmptyState` (`ui/EmptyState.tsx`, Rule 5): `variant="empty" | "filtered" | "error"`, with Clear filters (`onClearFilters`) or Try again (`onRetry`); `framed` for a whole tab. `EmptyCard` wraps it; `NoMatches` stays for older lists.
- `UndoLine` (`ui/UndoLine.tsx`, Rule 6): one plain line and a ghost Undo with the Undo2 icon, `busy` while undoing. `toast(type, message, { action: { label: 'Undo', onClick } })` for a result with no place on screen.
- `SaveBar` (`ui/SaveBar.tsx`, Rule 8): shown only while dirty, Discard then Save on the right, `placement="panel"` as a panel footer or `"screen"` pinned to the bottom; pair with `useDiscardGuard`.
- `IconButton` (`ui/IconButton.tsx`, Rule 11): sizes `sm` (28px) and `md` (32px), one radius, a required `label` that is its accessible name and Tooltip.
- `ShowMore` (`ui/ShowMore.tsx`, Rule 4): "Show 24 more of 76", nothing once all show.
- `Tabs` and `SubNav` (`ui/Tabs.tsx`, Rule 3): underline tabs for a page's sections (`tabId`, `tabPanelId` for the panels), segmented `SubNav` for two to four views. `ProjectTabs` wraps `Tabs`; `TabLayout` re-exports `SubNav`.
- `FilterButton`, `FilterSection`, `FilterOption` (`ui/FilterButton.tsx`, Rule 3): one trigger counting active filters, opening a panel of sections. `TaskFilterControl` is built on it.
- `Avatar` (`ui/Avatar.tsx`, Rule 11): one face for a person or an agent; `avatarFace()` gives the tone (one of `--color-avatar-1..8`, picked from the name, the id when there is none) and initials, and `AgentAvatar` uses the same function, adding its presence dot and ring.
- `ProviderLogo` (`ui/ProviderLogo.tsx`, Rule 20): the one logo bank. A provider's real mark by id or any name the app stores for it (`src/lib/logos.ts`: `LOGO_IDS`, aliases such as chatgpt and claude_code), only for providers the app supports; marks in `ui/logos/marks.ts` (single-color paths from Simple Icons, CC0; multicolor marks from the brands' own assets; every logo is its owner's trademark, used to identify the service). Sizes `xs` to `xl` on the type scale, `tile` for rows, `badge` (`done`, `waiting`, `problem`) in the bottom right and in the accessible name, `decorative` beside a visible name. Black or white marks sit on a quiet tile in the theme's ink; an unknown provider gets its initials. `ProviderPicker` (`ui/ProviderPicker.tsx`): the one way to choose a provider, logo tiles (or `ChoiceCard`s in a guided step) with an optional Other that reveals a name field; never a text box to type a provider.
- `PermissionGrid` (`src/components/access/PermissionGrid.tsx`, Rule 10): rows by area, columns View, View all and Manage, column titles select the column as far as the person may give, locked cells with the reason on hover, partial cells indeterminate, special powers listed below, a search box past 12 areas, a row of three labelled boxes per area on phones, `readOnly`. Controlled (`value`, `onChange`), with `limits` or `grantable` and `lockedReason`; logic in `src/lib/permission-grid.ts`.
- `src/lib/time-format.ts` (Rule 11): `formatAgo` ("Just now", "5 min ago", "2 h ago", "Yesterday", "Nov 26", "Nov 26, 2025"; `midSentence` for lower case), `formatDay`, `formatDateTime`, `formatFull` (tooltips) and `formatElapsed` ("under a minute", "16 min", "1 h 5 min").
- `page-read` (`globals.css`, Rule 9): the 880px reading width.
- `LineChart` (`ui/LineChart.tsx`, Rule 16): one brand series over time under a plain-words card title, light gridlines, three date labels, the unit on the axis, a plain tooltip (date and exact value) by pointer or arrow keys, and what will appear when empty.
- `npm run check:design` (`scripts/check-design.ts`): the banned markup (including glass-card padding other than p-4, p-5, lg:p-5, and a page body gap other than 6), against the ratchet baseline `scripts/design-baseline.json` (counts only go down; `-- --update-baseline` locks in a lower count; `-- --baseline-new-rules` once after adding a rule).

`StatStrip` is the one way to show a row of numbers: one surface (or `bare` inside a card), stats side by side with hairlines once its own width fits them all, a label and value list below that. Label 12px zinc-500 and value 20px semibold tabular in the row; label 14px zinc-300 and value 16px in the list. Props per stat: `label`, `value` (null shows `empty` muted), `context`, `tone` (default, success, warning, danger, info), `icon`, `href` or `onSelect` with `selected` (a brand underline), `spark`, `change` (against the last period: value, direction, and `good` when up or down has a meaning, green or red, neutral otherwise). ## Signature

- **Liquid glass on near-black**: translucent cards over a deep base, with a quiet teal glow reserved for what you can act on (`liquid-primary`). Glass blur is webkit-only by design.
- **Soft filled surfaces, never stripes**: state is a low-alpha fill (`bg-white/[0.04]`, `bg-amber-500/[0.14]`), never a left-border accent.
- **Pills that open cards**: the agent's work under a message is one line of small pills (Summary, 2 tasks, 1 reminder, Question for you) that each open a paragraph-width card, so nothing spreads across the page.
- **Plain, short words**: labels say what happens ("Add all 7", "Nothing to do", "Mark handled"). Results read as one plain line ("Added 7 suggestions"). Status and loader copy is three words, present tense. No em dashes.
- **Undo over confirm**: anything reversible acts at once and leaves one plain line with Undo in place; a confirmation is only for what cannot be undone.
- **Numbers in a row are one stat strip, never a grid of separate tiles** (Rule 16: on analytics pages its segments carry a trend line and pick the chart); on phones the strip becomes a label and value list. No odd stat stranded on its own row.
- **Built for a hundred**: lists of agents, assignments, brief entries and needs-you items search, filter and show a window with "Show 24 more of 76", never a row of one tile per agent.
- **Guided setups that create something with its own page are full pages** (an agent, a project): one step at a time, each checked before Next, with the same flow family everywhere. A short create flow that ends in a secret or a list row (an API key, a webhook endpoint) may be a step dialog; while a secret that is never shown again is on screen, only an explicit Done closes it (no backdrop, Escape or X).
- **Dense but calm**: 11 to 13px meta text, generous line height in reading areas (email bodies, transcripts), tabular numbers for times and counts.

## Moving to the rules

Screens built before 2026-11-26 are being moved onto the Rules above. Until a screen is moved, new work on it follows the Rules anyway; never copy an older screen's markup. The census of what differs today is the source for that work.
